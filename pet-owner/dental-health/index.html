It may be easier than you think to make a home dental routine stick.

Try pairing it with something you already do — in the morning, with exercise, while relaxing, or at bedtime. Would one of these work for you?

  3. EveniNg

  4. BedtimE

    Brushing teeth … Trying it with your pet too!

6 signs of dental disease

Talk to your veterinarian if you notice any of these signs.1

  • Bad breath

  • Discolored teeth

  • Excessive drooling

  • Red, swollen, bleeding gums

  • Facial swelling

  • Reluctance or difficulty eating

Why home dental care matters

Plaque starts small, but it can build up quickly — causing pain, inflammation and infection that results in tooth loss. And dental disease may affect more than just your pet’s smile. It can negatively impact their quality of life and cause serious health problems.1
