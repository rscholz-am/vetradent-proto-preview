FAQ

How long can the VETRADENT® Water Additive and Powder Water Additive be stored once they are mixed with water?

Prepare a fresh solution daily.

Do the VETRADENT® Water Additive and Powder Water Additive have to be refrigerated once mixed?

No. The mixed Water Additive and Powder Water Additive can be stored at any temperature that your pet prefers.

Can I use the VETRADENT® Water Additive and Powder Water Additive with a filtered drinking system?

Yes. The Water Additive and Powder Water Additive can be used with a filtered drinking system, even with charcoal filters.

How big are the VETRADENT® Dental Wipes?

The dental wipes are roughly the size of a standard facial tissue. The large size of the wipe makes it easier for pet owners to handle.

How often should I use the VETRADENT® Dental Spray on my dog or cat?

The spray should be applied twice daily. For cats and dogs less than 20 lbs, spray once on each side of the mouth. For dogs 20 lbs+, spray twice on each side of the mouth, coating teeth and gums.
