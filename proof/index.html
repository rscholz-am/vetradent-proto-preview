6 signs of dental disease

Talk to your veterinarian if you notice any of these signs.1

  • Bad breath

  • Discolored teeth

  • Excessive drooling

  • Red, swollen, bleeding gums

  • Facial swelling

  • Reluctance or difficulty eating

Why home dental care matters

Plaque starts small, but it can build up quickly — causing pain, inflammation and infection that results in tooth loss. And dental disease may affect more than just your pet’s smile. It can negatively impact their quality of life and cause serious health problems.1

FAQ

How long can the VETRADENT® Water and Powder Additives be stored once they are mixed with water?

Prepare a fresh solution daily.

Do the VETRADENT® Water and Powder Additives have to be refrigerated once mixed?

No. The mixed water and powder additives can be stored at any temperature that your pet prefers.

Can I use the VETRADENT® Water and Powder Additives with a filtered drinking system?

Yes. The water and powder additives can be used with a filtered drinking system, even with charcoal filters. The product will retain full efficacy.

How big are the VETRADENT® Dental Wipes?

The dental wipes are roughly the size of a standard facial tissue. The large size of the wipe makes it easier for pet owners to handle.

How often should I use the VETRADENT® Dental Spray on my dog or cat?

The spray should be applied twice daily. For cats and dogs less than 20 lbs, spray once on each side of the mouth. For dogs 20 lbs+, spray twice on each side of the mouth, coating teeth and gums.
