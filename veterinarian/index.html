Every VETRADENT® product features patented Biotrate® Technology to help reduce plaque and tartar and freshen breath.

A unique mode of action: How Biotrate® Technology works

The active ingredients in VETRADENT® immediately bind to the minerals found in a pet’s saliva, making them unavailable to calcify plaque into tartar.
