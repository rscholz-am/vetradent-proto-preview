Demonstrated Efficacy

59% tartar reduction1

VETRADENT® products are unique because they work to make it harder for plaque and tartar to accumulate by altering the mouth’s environment.

Dogs treated with VETRADENT® Dental Wipes had a 58.9% reduction in tartar scores, 37% reduction in gingivitis scores, and 17% reduction in plaque scores compared to dogs receiving no treatment.1

  • 59% less tartar
  • 37% less gingivitis
  • 17% less plaque

Also, VETRADENT® Water Additive showed significant improvements in tartar reduction.2

¹ Dental Wipes study meeting VOHC standards; study summary available on request.

² Water Additive clinical study; study summary available on request.

*Achieved in dogs only. VETRADENT® Water Additive and Powder Water Additive were achieved with once monthly brushing in accordance with the study design

Demonstrated Safety

From a third-party laboratory GSP study

Food grade ingredients safe for everyday consumption

Studies were conducted to evaluate the safety of VETRADENT® in 18 healthy adult dogs and 18 healthy adult cats. Made with Biotrate® patented technology, VETRADENT® uses food grade ingredients that are safe for everyday consumption. Please contact our Veterinary Technical Support Team for full safety information.

Reduced Halitosis

From a Dental Wipes study meeting VOHC standards

20% halitosis reduction

Without proper care, odor-producing bacteria can build up in a dog’s mouth. Dogs in a clinical study with VETRADENT® Dental Wipes experienced up to a 20% reduction in halitosis.

Biotrate® Technology

How VETRADENT® patented Biotrate® Technology works

The active ingredients in VETRADENT® bind to magnesium, calcium and iron found in the pet’s saliva, making them unavailable to calcify plaque into tartar.
